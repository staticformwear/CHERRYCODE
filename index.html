MEET THE FOUNDER:

A GRADUATE’S MISSION TO BUILD THE BOTTLE THAT LASTS.

Fountain Bottles was started by Michael, a graduate inspired to change the way we think about everyday bottles and create one built to last.

MEET THE OWNER

As a recent graduate, Michael noticed something frustrating about the bottles we use every day.

Too many simply weren’t built to last.

Fountain Bottles was created to change that.

The Problem:

We use our bottles every single day — at university, at work, at the gym, travelling and everywhere in between.

Yet so many eventually leak, break, lose their finish, or simply get replaced.

And when something is part of your everyday life, replacing it shouldn’t be the expectation.

There had to be a better way.

Fountain Bottles was going to be different.

How?

By creating a bottle built around one simple idea:

Make it once. Make it properly. Make it last.

Rather than creating another bottle designed to follow a trend, Fountain was created to be the one you reach for every day.

Reliable. Practical. Timeless.

But Why… Fountain?

Michael believed that choosing a reusable bottle shouldn’t mean compromising on quality.

If we want people to move away from disposable products, we need to create something they genuinely want to keep using.

Something that feels right in your hand.

Something you can take anywhere.

Something you can rely on day after day.

That became the mission behind Fountain Bottles:

Build a bottle worth keeping.

A bottle designed for everyday life, made with purpose, and built to stay with you for years to come.

Because ultimately, the best bottle is the one you don’t need to replace.

Today, Fountain Bottles continues that mission — creating drinkware designed to make reusable the easy choice.

One bottle. Less replacing. A better everyday choice.
